# CPT / AMA licensing — posture & good-faith record

_Internal record. **Not legal advice.** Before monetizing meaningfully or scaling,
get a short consult with an IP attorney to confirm whether the internal-use posture
below is sufficient or a CPT license is required._

## Our posture (as of 2026-06)
- **We do not display CPT codes or the AMA's CPT descriptors anywhere on the site.**
  Procedures are shown under our own plain-language names; the numeric codes were
  removed from all pages, structured data (JSON-LD), meta descriptions, and the
  client payloads (verified: 0 "CPT" in the built HTML).
- **Codes are used only internally**, as factual identifiers, to match line items
  in hospitals' **public, federally-mandated** machine-readable files (45 CFR §180)
  to the procedures we track. We do not redistribute the CPT code set or descriptors.
- We are **willing to comply** (license or adjust) if the AMA asks.
